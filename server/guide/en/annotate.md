# Annotate

## What to do here

- Choose where marks appear on the sheets and the header text.
- Choose how the files are saved and the symbols on the answer boxes, then preview one student to check.
- Annotate all sheets and download them.

## What this step is for

Making each student's answer sheet with their answers marked, the points per question and the final mark, to give back.

## How to do it

1. **On each sheet**: where the marks for each question go, and the header text at the top. In the header, `%s` is the mark, `%m` what it is out of, `%(ID)` the student's name, and `%(name)` or any other class list column its value.
2. **Files**: one PDF per student or one for the class, the file names, and whether to add the corrected question paper.
3. **Symbols and key**: how each answer box is marked. **Clear in black and white** (the default) gives each case its own shape, so the sheets stay readable when photocopied in greyscale; **AMC's colours** is AMC's red and blue; **Custom** chooses each shape and colour. The table shows each symbol as printed and in greyscale. The **key** that explains the symbols goes on every page, in the right margin or the top right corner, or is left out.
4. **Preview one student** to check the result in the preview panel.
5. Press **Annotate all sheets**, then **Download all (zip)**.

## Common problems

**File names show a copy number instead of a name.** That sheet isn't matched to a student. Go to Match students, then annotate again.

**The marks for each question cover the key.** They are written in both margins. Choose **In one margin** under Marks for each question, or put the key in the top right corner.

**Out of date.** The marks, matches or settings changed. Annotate again.
