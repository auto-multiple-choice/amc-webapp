# Mark

## What to do here

- Compute the marks once Check scans is clear: no unreadable pages, missing pages or pages to check.
- Marking always uses the latest marking scheme and answer key.
- Answers that break a question's rules, such as two boxes ticked on a single-answer question, are listed afterwards with a link to the page.
- Changed the marking scheme or a scan afterwards? The step shows "out of date": compute the marks again.

## What this step is for

Turning the ticked boxes into points and a final mark for every copy.

## How to do it

1. Check the list at the top: it should be all green.
2. Press **Compute marks**.

If problems remain, **Mark anyway** is still possible behind a warning; the warning says how each problem is treated.

## Common problems

**"Answers that break the rules".** A student ticked two boxes on a single-answer question, or "none of these" together with an answer. These score as an error (0 by default). Open the page to check that AMC read the boxes correctly.

**"Out of date".** Something the marks depend on changed. Compute them again.
