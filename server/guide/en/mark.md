# Mark

## What to do here

- Compute the marks once Check scans is clear: no unreadable pages, missing pages or pages to check.
- Marking always uses the latest marking scheme and answer key.
- Answers that break a question's rules, such as two boxes ticked on a single-answer question, are listed afterwards with a link to the page.
- Changed the marking scheme or a scan afterwards? The step shows "out of date": compute the marks again.
- After marking, **How each question did** shows each question's share right, how well it separates strong from weak students, and which answers were chosen.

## What this step is for

Turning the ticked boxes into points and a final mark for every copy.

## How to do it

1. Check the list at the top: it should be all green.
2. Press **Compute marks**.

If problems remain, **Mark anyway** is still possible behind a warning; the warning says how each problem is treated.

## How each question did

Once the marks are computed, a table lists every question:

- **Right**: the share of copies with full marks on it.
- **Separates**: the share right among the strongest 27% of copies (ranked by their total without this question) minus the share among the weakest 27%. 0.3 and more is good; under 0.15 the question tells strong and weak students apart poorly; below 0, weaker students did better than stronger ones, which often means a wrong answer key.
- Open a question to see how often each answer was chosen, overall and by the strongest and weakest copies, with the correct ones marked.
- **To look at** says in words what stands out: a very easy or very hard question, a key to check, a wrong answer that attracts strong students (check it isn't right too), or an answer nobody chose (it doesn't work as a wrong answer).

With fewer than 10 copies the figures say little. For questions that came from one of your banks, the figures are also kept with the bank's question, so it shows how the question did in every exam.

## Common problems

**"Answers that break the rules".** A student ticked two boxes on a single-answer question, or "none of these" together with an answer. These score as an error (0 by default). Open the page to check that AMC read the boxes correctly.

**"Out of date".** Something the marks depend on changed. Compute them again.
