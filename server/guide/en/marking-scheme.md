# Marking scheme

## What to do here

- Choose how single- and multiple-answer questions are scored by default.
- Set what the final mark is out of, and how it is rounded.
- Check each question's correct answers and points: click an answer to correct the answer key.
- All of this can still change after printing.

## What this step is for

How answers turn into points, and points into the final mark. Nothing here moves anything on the printed paper, so it all stays editable after printing.

## How to do it

**Default scoring** applies to every question without a rule of its own. Pick a preset, such as "1 point, −0.25 for a wrong answer" or "All or nothing", or write a rule in AMC's syntax: `b` is the score of a right answer, `m` of a wrong one, `haut=3` gives 3 points minus one per mistake, `mz=2` gives 2 points only for a perfect answer.

**Final mark**: the mark the total is scaled to (for example out of 20), how it's rounded, and the mark for a blank sheet.

**Questions** lists every question, with its answers, points and scoring:

- Click an answer to mark it correct or not. A single-answer question keeps exactly one correct answer.
- **Scoring…** sets a rule for one question, or neutralises it: **Full marks for everyone** (for a question that was wrong or unclear) or **Not counted**.
- **Count normally** puts a neutralised question back as it was.
- The total shows the points of a whole copy and the final mark it maps to.

## Common problems

**A question says "Edit in Questions".** The panel couldn't read it safely, for example because it's written with a macro. Change it in the source instead.

**Points show "?".** The question's rule uses a formula. AMC works out its points when marking, and the panel shows them after that.

**I changed something after marking.** Mark shows "out of date": compute the marks again, then export and annotate again.
