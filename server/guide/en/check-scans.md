# Check scans

## What to do here

- Unreadable pages: see what AMC saw, then remove them or scan them again.
- Missing pages: find and upload them.
- Pages to check: decide each box AMC wasn't sure about (T = ticked, N = not ticked, Enter = page done).
- Marks come next, once everything is clear.

## What this step is for

Settling what automatic reading couldn't, before the marks are computed.

## How to do it

- **Unreadable pages**: AMC couldn't find this exam's corner marks. **What AMC saw** shows why. Remove the page, or scan it again and upload it.
- **Missing pages**: some pages of a copy were scanned, others not. Find and upload them.
- **Pages to check**: boxes whose darkness is close to the limit are shown zoomed. Decide each with **T** (ticked) or **N** (not ticked), and press **Enter** when the page is done. The whole page is in the preview panel, where you can also click a box.
- **Accept the rest as detected** settles every remaining page as AMC read it.

## Common problems

**Almost every page is "to check".** The scans may be very light or dark. Under **Advanced**, adjust the detection sensitivity: a box counts as ticked when its darkness is between the two values.

**A student filled a box to cancel it.** A box darker than the upper value counts as cancelled, not ticked.

**I made a wrong decision.** Open the page from **All pages read** and use **Back to detected**, or decide again.
