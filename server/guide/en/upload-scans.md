# Upload scans

## What to do here

- Scan the answer sheets at 300 dpi, in black and white or greyscale.
- Upload the files (PDF or images). Each page is read automatically.
- Problems appear in the next step, Check scans.

## What this step is for

Each scanned page is matched to its copy and page, and every box is measured to see whether it was ticked.

## How to do it

1. Scan the sheets: 300 dpi, black and white or greyscale, straight.
2. Drag the files onto the upload area, or click it to choose them. Several files at once are fine.
3. Wait while AMC reads the pages.

If one printed paper was photocopied for the whole class, tick **Students wrote on photocopies** before the first upload.

## Common problems

**Many pages are unreadable.** Check the scan resolution (300 dpi), that pages are straight, and that the papers were printed at 100%.

**Photocopied sheets.** Without the photocopy option, copies of the same paper can't be told apart. The option can't change while scans exist: in Check scans, remove every uploaded page, then tick the option here and upload the scans again.

**Blank pages from a duplex scanner.** They show as unreadable. Remove them in Check scans.
