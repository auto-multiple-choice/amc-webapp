# Export marks

## What to do here

- Check the marks and how they are spread.
- Export to CSV (spreadsheets, school systems) or ODS (LibreOffice, with statistics).
- Export again after changing marks or matches.

## What this step is for

Getting the marks out of the app, for a spreadsheet or the school's system.

## How to do it

1. Look over the marks table and the histogram. Sort or search the table if needed.
2. Choose **CSV** or **ODS**, and the options: the order, ticked boxes (CSV), statistics (ODS), absent students.
3. Press **Export**. A CSV file opens in the preview panel; both can be downloaded.

The **shareable marks link** is for tools that import marks from an address. Anyone with the link can see student codes and marks without signing in, so turn it on only when needed.

## Common problems

**Names are missing.** Some sheets aren't matched to students yet. Go to Match students.

**The export is out of date.** The marks or matches changed after exporting. Export again.
